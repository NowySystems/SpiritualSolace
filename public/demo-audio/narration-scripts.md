# Care Binder narration scripts

Approved narration copy for the Care Binder guided demo. These scripts are provided so real ElevenLabs MP3 files can be generated consistently later.

Do not commit generated MP3 files until they are separately approved. The application should continue to run silently when audio files are absent.

## Expected audio scripts

### `care-queue-jane-doe.mp3`

Welcome to ChurchWork. Today we’ll follow Jane Doe, a demo resident receiving spiritual support through a coordinated care plan.

### `person-header.mp3`

Jane’s record keeps the person at the center, with her room, age, faith preference, and support context visible before anyone takes action.

### `current-need-next-safe-step.mp3`

Every care need is paired with a clear next safe step, so the team knows what to review before moving forward.

### `care-plan.mp3`

The spiritual care plan organizes prayer, visits, family encouragement, and church connection into one shared support record.

### `quick-actions.mp3`

Quick Actions let the care team send prayer requests, schedule visits, add follow-ups, contact churches, and record notes from one place.

### `send-prayer-request-action.mp3`

Prayer requests stay consent-confirmed and human-reviewed before they become part of the care timeline.

### `add-follow-up-action.mp3`

Follow-ups help make sure spiritual care needs are not forgotten after the first contact.

### `schedule-visit-action.mp3`

Visits can be scheduled through the same approved workflow, keeping support coordinated and visible.

### `contact-church-action.mp3`

Church contact uses consent-aware sharing, so outside coordination stays limited, respectful, and reviewed.

### `care-timeline.mp3`

Every care action becomes part of Jane’s timeline, creating continuity, accountability, and a clear story of support.

### `recent-activity.mp3`

Recent activity gives the team a quick view of what changed, who acted, and what still needs attention.
