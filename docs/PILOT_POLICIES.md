## No Medical Information Policy

ChurchWork is a spiritual care coordination tool.

ChurchWork is not a medical record, clinical communication system, emergency service, medical advice tool, treatment platform, or replacement for facility procedures.

Users may not submit, store, request, or share medical information in ChurchWork.

Forbidden information includes:

- Diagnosis.
- Symptoms.
- Medication.
- Treatment details.
- Medical history.
- Chart notes.
- Clinical instructions.
- Test results.
- Insurance information.
- Hospitalization details.
- Emergency information.

Requester/user path rule:

- Requesters cannot enter free-text notes.
- Requesters may only choose structured spiritual-care request options.

Facility and partner path rule:

- Facility and partner users may enter non-medical coordination notes only.
- Notes must be limited to spiritual-care priority, scheduling, prayer, visit coordination, follow-up, or care-action completion.

If medical information is entered by mistake:

1. The entry should be removed or redacted as soon as practical.
2. The event should be logged internally.
3. The user should be reminded of the no-medical-information rule.
4. Repeated misuse may result in access removal.

## Requester Acknowledgment

By submitting a request through ChurchWork, I understand and agree that:

- ChurchWork is for spiritual care coordination only.
- ChurchWork is not for emergencies.
- ChurchWork is not a medical record or clinical communication tool.
- I will not submit diagnosis, symptoms, medication, treatment details, medical history, chart notes, clinical instructions, insurance information, or emergency information.
- My structured spiritual-care request may be visible to facility users and approved care partners involved in the request.
- The facility and partner organization may use the request to coordinate prayer, pastoral visits, family support, church connection, or non-medical follow-up.

## Facility User Acknowledgment

As a facility user, I understand and agree that:

- ChurchWork is used only for spiritual care coordination.
- I may not enter medical chart information into ChurchWork.
- I may only add non-medical coordination notes.
- I am responsible for using ChurchWork according to facility policy.
- I will not share login credentials.
- I will only access requests connected to my assigned facility role.
- I will report suspected misuse, unauthorized access, or accidental medical-information entry.

## Partner User Confidentiality Acknowledgment

As a partner user, I understand and agree that:

- I may only access requests assigned or made visible to my partner organization.
- I may only use ChurchWork for spiritual care actions such as prayer, pastoral visit coordination, family support, church connection, or non-medical follow-up.
- I may not enter medical information.
- I may not copy, export, or share request information outside approved care coordination.
- I will follow facility visitor procedures.
- I will not share login credentials.
- I will report suspected misuse, unauthorized access, or accidental medical-information entry.

## Pilot Participation Notice

ChurchWork Pilot Safe v1 is an early controlled pilot between a facility and an approved partner organization.

Pilot limitations:

- The pilot may change as feedback is collected.
- ChurchWork does not guarantee a response time.
- ChurchWork is not for emergencies.
- ChurchWork is not a substitute for calling the facility, speaking with staff, or following facility procedures.
- ChurchWork does not provide medical advice or clinical services.
- Pilot access may be paused or revoked if needed for safety, security, privacy, or operational reasons.

## Privacy Policy working scope

ChurchWork may collect:

- Account name and email for authenticated users.
- Facility or partner organization membership.
- Structured spiritual-care request details.
- Request status.
- Timeline events.
- Non-medical coordination notes from facility and partner users.
- Policy acceptance records.
- Basic security/audit logs.

ChurchWork does not intentionally collect:

- Diagnosis.
- Symptoms.
- Medication.
- Treatment details.
- Medical history.
- Chart notes.
- Insurance information.
- Clinical instructions.
- Payment information inside the care workflow.

Data may be visible to:

- The requester, where applicable.
- Assigned facility users.
- Approved partner users, limited to partner-safe request context.
- ChurchWork owner/platform administrators for support, security, and pilot operations.

## Data Retention Policy draft

Default pilot retention posture:

- Keep active pilot data for the duration of the pilot.
- Retain audit and policy acceptance records long enough to support security review and operational accountability.
- Remove, redact, or archive data when no longer needed for pilot operation, legal obligations, security review, or agreed follow-up.
- Remove accidental medical information as soon as practical after discovery.

Specific retention windows should be finalized before expanding beyond Pilot #001.

## Incident Response Policy draft

A suspected security, privacy, or misuse incident includes:

- Unauthorized account access.
- Shared login credentials.
- Incorrect role access.
- Request visible to the wrong organization.
- Accidental medical information entry.
- Lost/stolen device with access to ChurchWork.
- Suspicious login or activity.

Initial response steps:

1. Pause or restrict affected access.
2. Preserve relevant audit logs.
3. Identify affected users, organizations, requests, and timeline events.
4. Remove or redact accidental medical information where possible.
5. Notify internal ChurchWork owners.
6. Coordinate with facility and partner contacts when appropriate.
7. Document what happened, what was affected, what was done, and what must change.

## Insurance posture for Pilot #001

Minimum target before or near live pilot launch:

- General Liability.
- Technology Errors & Omissions / Professional Liability.

Preferred upgrade trigger:

- Add cyber/privacy coverage when ChurchWork expands beyond one facility, signs paid contracts, handles higher request volume, or when a facility requests cyber coverage.
