# Donor Market Knowledge

## Purpose

Identify broad donor markets, sponsor groups, professional advisor networks, and philanthropic demographics that can help position the foundation as a high-impact place to give.

## Ethical Framing

The system should not build creepy individual dossiers. It should map donor markets and referral networks using aggregate, public, and approved data.

## Target Networks

- CPAs
- estate attorneys
- financial advisors
- trust officers
- bankers
- insurance agents
- business owners
- chambers of commerce
- civic clubs
- healthcare-adjacent businesses
- banks and credit unions
- utilities
- construction companies
- senior-care businesses
- medical suppliers

## Giving Vehicles

- QCD / RMD giving
- donor-advised funds
- charitable remainder trusts
- charitable lead trusts
- appreciated assets
- beneficiary designations
- sponsorships
- corporate giving
- foundation grants
- planned giving
