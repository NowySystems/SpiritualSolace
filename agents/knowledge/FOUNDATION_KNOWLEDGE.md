# Foundation Knowledge

## Organization

Cookeville Regional Charitable Foundation supports healthcare access and charitable assistance for Upper Cumberland residents.

## Mission Language

Building Healthier Communities.

## Service Area

The foundation assists patients and community needs across the 14 Upper Cumberland counties.
